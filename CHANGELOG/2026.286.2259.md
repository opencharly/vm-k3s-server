# 2026.286.2259 — fix(k3s-server): refuse to start k3s on an unsynchronised guest clock (#9) [successor to #10]

## Summary

The service-level guard for the chain whose root is fixed and merged (`opencharly/spec#219`): **k3s must not start on an unsynchronised guest clock**, because a freshly booted VM whose clock reads 1970 hands back certificates that read as "not yet valid" — the symptom this row documents (`last_update: Thu, 01 Jan 1970 00:00:43 +0000` in the guest's own cloud-init report).

An `exec_start_pre:` entry ahead of the existing ones, plus **both halves of its contract made assertable**. The second half took three review rounds to get right, which is why the reasoning is here and not only the result.

## How tested

**The bed exercises this branch's candy, and both new steps pass. This is the proof five review rounds asked for.**

```
$ CHARLY_REPO_OVERRIDE="github.com/opencharly/vm-k3s-server=<worktree>" charly -C . check run check-k3s-vm
  PASS  check the guest-clock guard is installed, executable, and does NOT refuse on a healthy clock  [plan:vm:k3s-vm:17]  exit=0
  PASS  check the guard REFUSES a guest whose clock reports unsynchronised, and says why              [plan:vm:k3s-vm:18]  exit=1
28 steps: 15 passed, 9 failed, 4 skipped
```

**Read the three numbers.** `28 steps` where every earlier run reported `26` — **the two new steps executed.** `15 passed` where the earlier runs passed `13` — **they passed.** And the `9 failed` are the *same nine* host-side `kube:` steps that fail on `main` for `#885`, unchanged and unrelated to this change.

**Both halves of a guard's contract are now asserted, and each check fails without the behaviour it guards:** delete the guard and step 17 fails (`exit_status: 0` gets no binary); delete the refusal arm and step 18 fails (the script would exit 0 where `1` is required). **A test that cannot fail is the defect `plugin-doctor#13` was filed about.**

**And for four rounds the bed could not see this branch at all.** The override needed its full bare ref — `github.com/opencharly/vm-k3s-server=…` — not a bare repo name. An unmatched key is silently ignored. I ran the bare name four times, and every conclusion I drew from "the override does not substitute" was wrong. **The documented form is in `charly/candy_source_dirs_override_test.go`; it has been correct the whole time.** Filed as `#884` with the diagnosis, including the real defect: *a key that matches nothing should say so.*
